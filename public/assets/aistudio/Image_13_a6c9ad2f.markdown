## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, premium, and reliable identity tailored for an Indonesian car rental and archipelago tour agency. It merges executive professionalism with the lush, vibrant spirit of trans-island travel.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Domestic corporate travelers, vacationing families, inbound international tourists, and luxury private chartered groups navigating major Indonesian destinations (Bali, Yogyakarta, Lombok, Jakarta, Labuan Bajo).
- **Emotional Response:** Utter reliability, pristine vehicle condition, safety, and seamless hospitality, framed in an aspirational outdoor aesthetic.

### Aesthetic Foundation: Corporate Modern & Translucent Nature
The UI blends refined corporate precision with refreshing hospitality cues:
- **Crisp Surfaces:** Clean white and cool slate foundations reminiscent of high-end automotive dashboards and airline booking terminals.
- **Natural Depth:** Deep emerald tones evoking Indonesian rainforests and volcanic vistas, balanced against crisp mint highlights for instant visual energy.
- **Structured Density:** Balanced data presentation displaying fleet specs (transmission, passenger capacity, luggage volume) and transparent Rupiah pricing without clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep emerald tones with high-clarity neutrals to communicate safety, speed, and luxury.

### Role Mapping
- **Primary (`#059669` - Emerald 600):** The primary brand anchor used for primary booking CTAs, active vehicle filter pills, and selected date ranges.
- **Secondary (`#10b981` - Emerald 500):** Vibrant interaction state for hover targets, active progression indicators, and instant availability badges.
- **Tertiary (`#14b8a6` - Teal 500):** Complementary accent for special discount indicators, tour highlights, and WhatsApp concierge touchpoints.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0f172a` - Slate 900):** Deepest structural base for high-contrast headlines, master headers, and footer components.
- **Neutral Slate Surfaces:**
  - `#1e293b` (Slate 800) and `#334155` (Slate 700) for secondary body copy, icons, and muted borders.
  - `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) and `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100) for vehicle catalog canvas, input field fills, and alternating specification tables.
  - `#ffffff` (Crisp Pure White) for elevated booking cards and focused interactive sheets.
- **Semantic Indonesian Currency Accent:** IDR pricing tokens utilize `#064e3b` (Emerald 900) for baseline currency symbols with `#059669` numerals to maintain immediate financial legibility.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a modern humanist typeface crafted in Indonesia that provides international precision with localized cultural resonance.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (`700` and `800`) paired with subtle negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) mimic contemporary European automotive brochures and premium booking platforms.
- **Fleet Specifications:** Technical specs (e.g., *2.4L Diesel*, *Manual/Matic*, *7 Seater*) use `label-md` or `body-sm` with neutral slate tones (`#334155`) to prioritize scannability.
- **Rupiah Formatting:** The `price-display` level is explicitly reserved for IDR amounts (e.g., `Rp 650.000 / hari`). The currency prefix `Rp` is set 2 sizes smaller or matched with `label-lg` baseline-aligned to the integer to ensure prominence without visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt grid drives rhythmic composition across landing screens, vehicle grids, and tour itineraries.

### Grid Construction
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column responsive grid with `margin-desktop` (80px), `gutter` (24px), and a max-width container of `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column responsive grid with `margin` (32px) and `gutter` (24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `margin-mobile` (16px) and `gutter-mobile` (16px).

### Section Rhythms
- **Hero & Fleet Grid Padding:** Section spacing leverages `space-xl` multiplied by 2 (80px) on desktop to establish high-end breathing room, compressing to 48px on mobile.
- **Search & Booking Bar:** Floats between the hero banner and catalog using negative top margins, maintaining `space-md` internal component gaps for rapid date, city, and driver toggling.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, employing crisp architectural layering and soft tinted glows reminiscent of glass and tropical daylight.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#f8fafc` (Slate 50). Provides high-contrast separation from vehicle cards and imagery.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Pure `#ffffff` framed by subtle hairline borders (`1px solid #e2e8f0`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Booking Widget & Modals):** Pure `#ffffff` supported by an ambient diffused shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Header & Nav):** Translucent `#ffffff` at 85% opacity with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.

### Border & Elevation Interaction
Interactive cards elevate from `Surface Level 1` to `Level 2` on hover with a smooth 200ms ease curve, shifting the border color from slate-200 (`#e2e8f0`) to emerald-500/30 (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** profile (8px standard corner radius), offering a refined balance of corporate trust and contemporary softness.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Standard base radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Vehicle & Tour Package Cards:** `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px) for approachable product framing.
- **Hero Visual Containers & Large Panels:** `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Pill Badges (Exceptions):** Specification chips (e.g., fuel type, transmission, driver options) and status badges utilize full pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create distinction against rectilinear car cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Now, Search Fleet):** Solid emerald background (`#059669`), white text (`#ffffff`), `font-weight: 600`, 8px corner radius. On hover: shifts to `#10b981` with subtle vertical translation (`translate-y(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action (View Details, Download Itinerary):** Pure white background, 1px border (`#cbd5e1`), slate-800 text (`#1e293b`). On hover: border transitions to `#059669` with emerald-50 tint (`#ecfdf5`).
- **WhatsApp Concierge Button:** Vibrant emerald/teal tone (`#10b981`), rounded pill shape, accompanied by a 20px chat icon for instant direct Indonesian customer service.

### Chips & Specification Badges
- **Specification Badges:** Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`), slate-100 background (`#f1f5f9`), slate-700 text (`#334155`), with small inline monochrome SVG icons (e.g., transmission, luggage, passenger icons).
- **Availability & Pricing Badges:** Soft emerald-50 background (`#ecfdf5`), emerald-700 text (`#047857`), border `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`.

### Vehicle & Tour Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white surface (`#ffffff`), border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, `16px` border radius, and `16px` inner padding.
- **Content Hierarchy:** 
  1. Top metadata: Vehicle category badge (e.g., "Premium MPV") and instant status.
  2. Vehicle render/photography: Contained aspect ratio (16:10) on neutral background.
  3. Vehicle Model: `headline-sm` in `#0f172a`.
  4. Horizontal pill strip: Capacity, gear type, air conditioning, fuel.
  5. Bottom bar: Clear price breakdown (`Rp 750.000 / hari` with driver inclusion indicator) and Primary CTA button.

### Form Inputs & Search Module
- **Floating Search Engine:** Compact horizontal unit containing 4 core dropdown segments: "Lokasi Penjemputan" (Pickup Location), "Tanggal Sewa" (Rental Dates), "Layanan" (Lepas Kunci vs. Dengan Supir), and "Cari Mobil" CTA.
- **Input Fields:** Crisp 1px border (`#cbd5e1`), `#f8fafc` background, transitioning to pure white background with a 2px emerald-600 focus ring (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`).

### Toggles (Lepas Kunci vs. Dengan Supir)
- Segmented control enclosed in a slate-100 container (`#f1f5f9`) with an emerald-600 active sliding pill indicating self-drive ("Lepas Kunci") versus all-inclusive chauffeur service ("Dengan Supir").
