## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, bespoke hospitality, and modern Indonesian luxury. Designed for high-net-worth travelers, diplomatic delegations, and executive clientele booking chauffeured private transfers and luxury car rentals across the archipelago, the aesthetic rejects hyper-digital gloss and saturated neon in favor of an understated, tactile matte finish.

The aesthetic fuses architectural minimalism with heritage warmth. Deep charcoal obsidian surfaces evoke polished volcanic basalt, grounded by brushed bronze and muted antique gold highlights that reflect traditional Indonesian craftsmanship without theatrical glare. The interface prioritizes spaciousness, disciplined restraint, and pristine typography, ensuring interactions feel composed, effortless, and distinguished.

## Colors

The color palette centers entirely on low-reflectance, high-fidelity dark tones:

- **Neutral Canvas (`#12161A`)**: Deep Charcoal Obsidian forms the unyielding foundation, eliminating pure black glare while offering rich depth for midnight browsing.
- **Card & Container Surfaces (`#1E232A`)**: Matte Graphite creates structural separation. Surfaces avoid gloss, relying on flat matte saturation to nest content.
- **Primary Accent (`#C5A059`)**: Brushed Bronze and Muted Gold serves as the focal driver for key interactive triggers, status indicators, and luxury badges. It is intentionally desaturated and velvety, avoiding metallic mirror shine.
- **Secondary Structural Tone (`#2A323D`)**: An elevated graphite used exclusively for delicate borders, input outlines, and dividers.
- **Typography & Glyphs**: Primary reading text utilizes Soft Silver (`#E2E8F0`) to ensure crisp readability without eye strain against obsidian backgrounds. Metadata, captions, and deactivated states employ Subtle Slate (`#94A3B8`).

## Typography

The type system pairs **Newsreader** for editorial display headlines with **Manrope** for analytical, legibility-first interface reading:

- **Newsreader** imbues vehicle showcase titles, editorial quotes, and regional itinerary features with the authoritative elegance of bespoke travel journals. It brings literary cadence and warmth to an otherwise dark technical canvas.
- **Manrope** provides contemporary balance, structural precision, and high legibility at micro sizes. Its geometric, low-contrast letterforms keep technical vehicle specifications (engine capacities, seating layouts, transfer rates) clear and effortless to process.
- All label and badge typography enforces uppercase casing with expanded letter-spacing (0.08emâ€“0.12em) to mimic luxury automotive badging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single or dual-column structural format on mobile. The spacing model emphasizes rhythmic vertical cadence and generous horizontal margins to preserve a bespoke, unhurried atmosphere.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column flow with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Vehicle inspection cards and fleet selectors scroll horizontally with trailing peek margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column flow with `margin-tablet: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
